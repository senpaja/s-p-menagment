FAQ

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Want to know more before applying? Start here, or reach out to our team during the application process.

Ask us directly ↗
What does a chatter do?+

You manage text conversations on creator accounts, building rapport and helping fans find content they’ll enjoy. You work behind the scenes and never appear on camera.

Do I need experience?+

No prior experience is required. We look for strong written English, reliability, a computer, and a willingness to learn. Training and guidelines are provided.

What are the hours?+

The role is full time, with fixed eight-hour shifts on Central European time. The available shift options are 04:00–12:00, 12:00–20:00, and 20:00–04:00.

How does compensation work?+

Compensation is performance-based, with monthly payouts on the 28th. We explain the commission structure and terms clearly during the hiring process.

Do I need to create content or use my own profile?+

No. The work is text-only and behind the account. You do not create content or use a personal social profile.

How long does hiring take?+

We aim to review applications within 24 hours. Skills check, conversation with the team, and onboarding can follow within days, depending on availability.
